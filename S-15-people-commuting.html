People & commuting
Emissions from staff travelling between home and workplace, and electricity used when working from home. Scope 3 Category 7 — employee commuting.
HR manager / admin
0 of 3 sections completed
Scope boundary: This cluster covers daily home→workplace→home travel by all staff (Cat 7). Business trips during work hours go in S-11 Transport & fleet (Cat 6). If you are a home-based business, your own work electricity goes in S-10 Power & energy.
Where does this data come from? Commuting data isn't on any bill. The most practical approach: a brief staff survey asking each person their usual transport mode and one-way distance. Alternatively use the estimation method below — enter total staff per mode with Singapore average distances. GHG Protocol permits reasonable estimation for Cat 7.
Cat 7 Public transport commuting — MRT, LRT, bus —
Staff commuting by MRT, LRT, or public bus
?
Daily home-to-work-and-back travel by public transport. Singapore's MRT is the lowest-carbon commute mode — far below private cars.
Public transport emissions come from electricity (MRT) and diesel (buses) allocated per passenger-km. Even with Singapore's current grid, MRT emits about one-quarter of a private car per km travelled.
🚇 MRT: 0.042 kgCO₂/km · Bus: 0.074 kgCO₂/km · Petrol car: 0.173 kgCO₂/km — MRT is 4× cleaner than driving
SEFR: Land Transport → MRT/LRT: 0.0416 kgCO₂/person-km · Public Bus: 0.0737 kgCO₂/person-km · Source: MOT Singapore

Cat 7 Private vehicle commuting — car, motorcycle —
Staff commuting by private car or motorcycle
?
Staff who drive or ride their own vehicles to work. Higher emission factor than public transport — a key lever if your workplace is well-served by MRT.
Private cars emit 4× more per km than MRT. For staff in industrial areas (Tuas, Jurong, Senoko) or on shift work, private vehicles may be the only practical option. Motorcycles emit roughly half a car per km.
🚗 1 staff member driving 12 km each way, 22 days/month ≈ 0.87 tCO₂e/year from commuting alone
SEFR: Land Transport → ICE Car: 0.1732 · Hybrid: 0.1343 · EV: 0.0618 · Motorcycle: 0.0834 kgCO₂/person-km · Source: MOT Singapore

Cat 7 Work from home — staff electricity —
Staff WFH electricity optional
?
When staff work from home, they use electricity for their work equipment. This is part of your company's Scope 3 Cat 7 footprint — even though it appears on employees' personal bills. Only business-use electricity counts, not the household's full consumption.
GHG Protocol includes WFH electricity as Cat 7 because it is caused by company work activities, even if consumed at the employee's home. Emission factor is Singapore's EMA Grid EF (0.412 kgCO₂/kWh). Only the business-use proportion is included — the same equipment wattage methodology used in S-10.
🏠 5 staff WFH 8 days/month with computer + fan (85W), 8 hrs/day = 27.2 kWh/month · 326 kWh/year · 0.134 tCO₂e/year — small but visible at scale
EMA Grid EF 2023: 0.412 kgCO₂/kWh · Business-use equipment wattage only · GHG Protocol Scope 3 Cat 7
