Section 2
About this report

This report covers ZYT Solutions Pte Ltd's Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) performance for the calendar year ending 31 December 2024. It has been prepared with reference to SGX Core ESG Metrics (April 2023) and the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard.

Greenhouse gas emissions have been measured using the operational control method as defined by the GHG Protocol. All emission factors are sourced from Singapore's official Emission Factors Registry (SEFR), published by the National Environment Agency (NEA), January 2026 edition. The Singapore grid emission factor of 0.412 kgCO₂/kWh (EMA, 2023) is applied to all purchased electricity.

This report has not been externally assured. It constitutes a self-declaration of ESG performance in accordance with the stated frameworks.

Section 8 — Environmental
Targets and commitments

Based on our 2024 baseline, we have committed to the following emissions reduction targets:

We commit to reducing our total Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 25% by 2027, from our 2024 baseline of 36.0 tCO₂e to 27.0 tCO₂e.
Scope 1+2 · 3-year target · Annual saving: est. SGD 9,675

Section 9 — Environmental
Actions taken

This is our first reporting year (2024). The establishment of our emissions baseline is itself a significant operational step — it enables evidence-based decision-making on energy, transport, and waste that was not previously possible.

In the coming reporting period, we will use this baseline to identify and implement targeted reduction actions across our highest-emission clusters. Progress against our committed targets will be reported in our 2025 Sustainability Report.
