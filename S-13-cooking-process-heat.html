Scope 1 Piped gas (metered supply) —
Piped gas consumption
?
Natural gas distributed through Singapore's piped network by your gas supplier (SembCorp Gas, Keppel Gas, Union Energy, or others). Used for cooking, water heating, steam generation, and industrial processes. Your gas bill shows consumption in kWh.
Piped gas in Singapore is primarily natural gas (methane) distributed by various licensed suppliers. Combustion releases CO₂ directly — this is a Scope 1 emission because you own the combustion appliance. Natural gas is the cleanest-burning fossil fuel per unit of energy, but it still generates significant emissions at commercial cooking scale.
🔥 1 kWh of gas ≈ 0.202 kgCO₂e · A commercial kitchen burning 5,000 kWh/month = 1.01 tCO₂e/month from gas alone

Scope 1 LPG cylinders & bulk LPG —
LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) consumption
?
LPG cylinders used for cooking, heating, or industrial processes. Common in hawker centres, food courts, catering, and areas without piped gas connections.
LPG (propane/butane mix) is stored in cylinders and delivered to premises. It's slightly more carbon-intensive than natural gas per unit of energy, but the difference is small. The key is knowing how many cylinders you use — often tracked by the number of deliveries.
🧴 One standard 12kg cylinder ≈ 35.8 kgCO₂e · One commercial 48kg cylinder ≈ 143.4 kgCO₂e · A hawker stall using 4 cylinders/month = 143 kgCO₂e/month
SEFR: Fuel → Combustion → Liquefied Petroleum Gas → 2,987.208 kgCO₂e/metric ton · Per kg: 2.987 kgCO₂e/kg · Per litre: 2.987 × 0.55 kg/L = 1.643 kgCO₂e/L

Scope 1 Industrial boiler / process heat (diesel, fuel oil) —
Boiler or industrial process heat optional
?
Diesel or fuel oil burned in an industrial boiler for steam generation, hot water, laundry, or industrial process heat. Common in food manufacturing, hotels, laundry services, and hospitals.
Industrial boilers burn diesel or fuel oil to generate steam or hot water for manufacturing processes, commercial laundry, kitchen steamers, and space heating. These are high-volume fuel consumers — a medium boiler can burn 200–500 litres of diesel per day. Check with your engineering or facilities team for fuel consumption data.
⚙️ A medium commercial boiler burning 10 litres/hr × 8 hrs/day × 22 days = 1,760 L/month ≈ 4.7 tCO₂e/month

Scope 1 Charcoal & other solid fuels —
Charcoal, wood, or other solid fuels optional
?
Charcoal or wood burning for BBQ restaurants, traditional cooking, satay, or steamboat. Less common but relevant for specific F&B formats.
Charcoal has a relatively low GHG emission factor per kg compared to fossil fuels — but the quantities used in commercial settings can add up. SEFR classifies charcoal under the fossil fuel category, so emissions are counted. If you use certified sustainable wood/biomass, the IPCC allows a near-zero emissions factor (biogenic carbon) — but this requires documentation.
🍢 A satay stall using 20 kg of charcoal per day × 25 days = 500 kg/month ≈ 98 kgCO₂e/month (relatively low compared to gas)
SEFR: Fuel → Combustion → Charcoal: 196.47 kgCO₂e/metric ton = 0.196 kgCO₂e/kg · Wood/wood waste: 29.64 kgCO₂e/metric ton = 0.030 kgCO₂e/kg
