Solar panels — on-site generation optional
?
If your business has solar panels, enter the monthly generation consumed on-site. This offsets your grid electricity emissions.
On-site solar generation has zero Scope 2 emissions under GHG Protocol's market-based method. It directly offsets your grid electricity consumption. Enter only what you consume on-site — exclude any surplus sold back to the grid via EMA's REBS scheme.
☀️ A 10kWp commercial rooftop system generates ~12,000 kWh/year in Singapore — saving ~4.9 tCO₂e and approximately SGD 3,600/year in electricity cost
GHG Protocol: Market-based Scope 2 · Self-generated solar = 0 kgCO₂e/kWh
🚫 HDB flat: Solar panels are not approved for installation in HDB flats by HDB. This section does not apply.
⚠️ Commercial tenant: Installing solar panels requires landlord consent and EMA/BCA approval. Check with your building owner before proceeding. If you don't have solar, mark as N/A below.
✅ Own premises: Solar installation is encouraged. EMA's SolarNova programme and ENGIE/Sunseap offer turnkey solutions for commercial properties. Consider this as a reduction action after completing your baseline.

District cooling system optional
?
Centralised chilled water supply — applicable in Marina Bay, Jurong Lake District, and select business parks.
District cooling plants use electricity to generate chilled water for multiple buildings. Your bill shows cooling consumed in RT-hr or kWh thermal. We convert to electricity using the plant's efficiency (kW/RT), then apply the EMA grid emission factor.
🏢 Mainly relevant for tenants in Marina Bay Financial Centre, Marina One, Asia Square, Jurong Lake District
Formula: RT-hr × kW/RT × 0.412 kgCO₂/kWh · kW/RT = 3.517 ÷ COP

Diesel consumed in generator
?
Diesel burned in a backup or primary generator on your premises. This is a direct Scope 1 emission — you own the source.
Burning diesel directly releases CO₂, CH₄, and N₂O — all three greenhouse gases expressed as CO₂-equivalent. If you only use your generator during power outages, annual consumption may be very low.
⛽ 1 litre of diesel ≈ driving 12 km in Singapore traffic, or running a 5kVA generator for ~1 hour
