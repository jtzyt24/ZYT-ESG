Cat 1 Cloud services — AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, others —
Cloud storage & compute · Scope 3 Cat 1 ?
Storage, virtual machines, databases, and other cloud services. Emissions come from electricity used in data centres. Singapore's major cloud providers all operate regional data centres using Singapore's grid.
Cloud providers publish carbon footprint tools and emission factors. For Singapore-hosted workloads, IMDA/NUS ESI published factors apply (0.412 kgCO₂/kWh grid EF). For global/multi-region providers, location-specific factors vary. The most accurate approach is to use the provider's own carbon footprint report.
SEFR/IMDA: Cloud storage (hot tier) ~0.0000335 kgCO₂/GB/month · VM general purpose ~0.000533 kgCO₂/instance-hour · Source: NUS ESI / IMDA Singapore

Cat 2 IT hardware purchases — computers, servers, phones —
Capital IT equipment purchased this year optional ?
Laptops, desktops, servers, printers, phones, and other IT hardware purchased during the reporting year. Emissions come from manufacturing — a one-time cost allocated to the year of purchase.
IT hardware manufacturing is Scope 3 Cat 2 (capital goods). The emission factor covers the full manufacturing lifecycle — materials, fabrication, and transport to point of sale. For a laptop this is typically 300–400 kg CO₂e. For a server: 500–2,000 kg CO₂e. These are one-time emissions in the year of purchase.
GHG Protocol Cat 2: Laptop ~350 kgCO₂e · Desktop ~500 kgCO₂e · Server ~1,200 kgCO₂e · Smartphone ~70 kgCO₂e · Source: manufacturer EPDs and IEA lifecycle data

Cat 1 Co-location & hosted servers —
Co-located or hosted servers optional ?
Physical servers hosted in a third-party data centre (Equinix, Keppel DC, Starhub, etc.) that you pay for by rack space or server instance. Different from cloud — you own or lease the hardware.
Co-located servers draw electricity from the data centre, which charges you for rack space. The emission is based on your power draw × the data centre's PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) × Singapore's grid factor. Most Singapore Tier 3+ data centres have PUE of 1.3–1.5.
Formula: Monthly kWh × PUE × 0.412 kgCO₂/kWh · Or: monthly spend × spend-based factor
kWh / month
PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness): Tier 3 SG data centres typically 1.3–1.5. Check with your data centre provider.
