Procurement & supply chain
Packaging, cleaning materials, outsourced services, and other purchased goods. Scope 3 Cat 1 — emissions from producing the goods and services you buy. Spend-based approach is the standard method for SMEs without supplier-level data.

Methodology: This cluster uses spend-based emission factors (PACT-3.0 / EEIO methodology) — the standard approach when supplier-specific data is unavailable. Accuracy improves with actual spend data from your accounts. GHG Protocol permits spend-based factors for Cat 1 at SME level.
Cat 1 Packaging materials —
Packaging purchased for products / orders ?
Boxes, bags, bubble wrap, tape, labels, and other packaging materials used in your operations. Emissions come from raw material extraction and manufacturing of the packaging.
Packaging emissions depend heavily on material type. Cardboard/paper is the most common for Singapore retail and F&B. Plastic packaging has a higher carbon intensity. The spend-based factor converts your annual purchasing spend into an estimated emission.
EEIO spend-based: Paper/cardboard packaging ~0.45 kgCO₂/SGD · Plastic packaging ~0.60 kgCO₂/SGD · Mixed packaging ~0.50 kgCO₂/SGD · Source: PACT-3.0 SEA factors

Cat 1 Cleaning & facility services —
Outsourced cleaning, security, facility management optional
Third-party cleaning services, security guards, pest control, facility management contracts. The emissions from providing these services — including the cleaners' travel, chemicals, and equipment — are part of your supply chain footprint.
SGD / yr

Cat 1 Professional & business services —
Accounting, legal, marketing, IT services optional
Outsourced professional services that support your operations. These have a relatively low emission intensity per dollar spent — primarily from office electricity and staff commuting at the service provider.
SGD / yr
Includes accounting fees, legal retainers, agency fees, IT support contracts. EEIO factor: ~0.08 kgCO₂/SGD for professional services.
